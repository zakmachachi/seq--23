Only windows with at least two oscillator cycles and a contributing path above −60 dB are shown. Components are projected onto the single body oscillator phase and its harmonics. A clean sine has no second or third harmonic; these projections can show window leakage and are excluded from the fundamental summary. The swept body is not stationary in the onset window; short-window leakage may affect the projection. The dirty high-pass protects the bass by keeping its return small there, regardless of phase. Gate-off windows are measured before the deliberate output gate. A phase angle alone does not establish cancellation: relative level matters too. These are sampled combinations, not an exhaustive proof or a prediction of a soundsystem's acoustic response.
